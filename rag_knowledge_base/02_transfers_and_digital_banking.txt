================================================================================
RED SEA LAB BANK - KNOWLEDGE BASE: TRANSFERS & DIGITAL BANKING
================================================================================

DOCUMENT OVERVIEW:
This document details money transfer protocols, digital banking features, mobile app capabilities, security standards, and API routing specs for Red Sea Lab Bank.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MONEY TRANSFER SERVICES & LIMITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 Internal Transfers & Microservice API
- Endpoint: /api/v1/transfer
- Description: Instant transfers between Red Sea Lab Bank accounts or to other registered Red Sea Lab Bank members.
- Execution Time: Instant (Real-time 24/7/365).
- Fees: $0 for all accounts.

1.2 Wire Transfers (Domestic & International)
- Domestic Wires: Processed same-day if submitted before 4:00 PM EST.
  * Fee: $0 for Premium Tier / $15 for Standard Tier.
- International Wires: Processed within 1 to 2 business days via SWIFT network.
  * Fee: $0 for Premium Tier / $30 for Standard Tier.
- Cutoff Time: 4:00 PM EST Monday through Friday (excluding federal holidays).

1.3 ACH Direct Transfers
- External Bank Transfers: 1 to 2 business days standard processing.
- Daily Limits: $10,000 per day for Standard Tier / $50,000 per day for Premium Tier.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DIGITAL BANKING & MOBILE APP FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 Web Portal & Mobile Access
- Banking Dashboard: Real-time portfolio tracking, balance overview, transaction history export (CSV/PDF).
- Direct Deposit: Instant payroll access up to 2 days early with qualified direct deposit.
- Mobile Check Deposit: Snap pictures of paper checks for instant deposit credit up to $5,000 daily.

2.2 Flowise AI Banking Assistant Integration
- Embedded Chatbot Widget: Powered by Flowise AI and Qdrant vector database.
- Capabilities: Answers banking inquiries, checks transfer rules, explains account tiers, assists with referral programs, and guides users through digital services.
- Endpoint Host: https://flowise-local-ai-lab.apps.ai-guardrails.bd.f5.com
- Security: Privacy-first guardrails ensure user credentials and sensitive PII are never logged or stored by external AI models.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SECURITY, AUTHENTICATION & MULTI-FACTOR AUTH (MFA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Encryption: 256-bit AES encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.
- Session Protection: Authenticated sessions use secure client-side sessionStorage and automated timeout after 15 minutes of inactivity.
- OpenShift Non-Root Security: Hosted on Red Hat OpenShift using unprivileged containers (port 8080) for maximum security isolation.
